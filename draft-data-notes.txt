Great Mates FFL draft preview 02 — data and behaviour

Updated 2 October 2026 for the 2027 draft preview.

Logo: embedded logo from the existing FFL website header.
Team shortnames: sandbox FFL_WebApp_Data, Users_Teams columns C:D.
Draft order is illustrative, not the approved 2027 draft order.

Players: 60 distinct players selected using saved 2026 Player Stats workbook
Round 0 2026 data through Round 24 2026 data, columns A:Q. Finals excluded.
Scoring is calculated for each game, then averaged over recorded games.
No minimum games cutoff. Ties: games played descending, then player name.
The top 10 for each of 6 roles contain 47 distinct players. Starting at rank
11, visit Forward, Ruck, Marker, Midfielder, Tackler, All Rounder in order,
adding unseen players until the pool contains 60.

Scoring agrees with the supplied FFL 2027 Competition League Rules and
sandbox Season workbook Master A19:M28 / Rd01 R4:R10:
Forward = 6*goals + 2*behinds
Ruck = hitouts + 3*goals
Marker = 3*marks + kicks + 3*goals
Midfielder = disposals + max(0, disposals-29) + 3*goals
Tackler = 3*tackles + handballs + 3*goals
All Rounder = disposals + tackles + marks + 3*goals

These are calculated saved-source averages, not invented sample values.
The saved scraper results have not been independently audited for completeness
or accuracy against the AFL. Club names reflect this saved source.

Drafted players stay searchable and viewable with team and round.pick.
They remain crossed out in the queue until explicitly removed. Draft actions
are disabled for taken players. Six demo rounds / 60 picks are available.

This is a single-browser commissioner demo. Picks and queue persist locally.
It does not synchronize between coaches, use the Apps Script API, change
league spreadsheets or send email. Shared draft integration remains pending.
